# Security

To report a security issue in nolanlabs or on nolanlabs.io, email **[hello@nolanlabs.io](mailto:hello@nolanlabs.io)** with “Security” in the subject line. Please don’t open a public issue.

Include what you found, where (a URL or request), and the steps to reproduce it. A person on the team reads every report and replies to you directly.

Please keep the details private until we’ve fixed the issue, and don’t access, change or delete data that isn’t yours while testing.

This policy is also linked from [nolanlabs.io/.well-known/security.txt](https://nolanlabs.io/.well-known/security.txt).
